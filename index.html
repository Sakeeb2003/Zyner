Maldives Escape

7 Days in Paradise — Maldives Luxury Getaway

North Malé Atoll, Maldives · 7 Days / 6 Nights

Limited Offer $899 / person (Save 25%)

Package Overview

Escape to the world's premier tropical island destination. Stay in a luxury overwater villa with direct ocean access, private seaplane transfers, gourmet dining over the water, and guided coral reef snorkeling adventures.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Arrival & Scenic Seaplane Transfer

Welcome at Velana International Airport followed by a breath-taking 30-minute seaplane flight to your private resort island.

Day 2–3
Overwater Villa Stay & Coral Reef Snorkeling

Unwind in your ocean villa. Enjoy guided house-reef snorkeling, sea kayaking, and private beach relaxation.

Day 4–5
Sunset Dolphin Cruise & Overwater Spa

Take a traditional Dhoni sailboat cruise to spot wild dolphins at sunset, followed by a complimentary couples spa session.

Day 6–7
Candlelight Beach Dinner & Farewell Departure

Indulge in a 4-course seaside dinner under the stars, followed by breakfast and your return seaplane transfer.

What's Included

  • 6 Nights Luxury Overwater Villa

  • Daily Gourmet Breakfast & Dinner
  • Guided Coral Snorkeling Tour
  • Sunset Dolphin Cruise Pass
